Generated by `make eval`. Each mutation is applied to an unmodified upstream
Nav2 config from `075b29611a`; expectations were recorded in
`eval/mutations.yaml` before the harness ran and are never edited to match
the outcome.

Detection rate is computed over every hazardous mutation, including those

blind spot; excusing it would turn this report into an advertisement.

## History of this catalogue

The first run reported 6 of 19 hazards detected, with two wrong priors, and it turned up three defects — two in the tool and one in this harness. In the tool: M13 (a motion limit deleted from one side of the comparison) and M15 (the same key declared twice with different values) both stayed silent where the catalogue expected UNKNOWN. Neither was a capability gap; both violated the existing rule that EAL must not report a clean result for input it did not analyze, and both were fixed by adding the INCOMPLETE_COMPARISON and AMBIGUOUS_INPUT coverage gaps. In the harness: detection was measured by diffing finding *categories* against the baseline, which cannot see a mutation that worsens an existing finding. M09 weakens braking sevenfold and was scored as silent because the category was already present in the baseline. Diffing now uses category plus title. That bug made the tool look worse than it is, but it was still a broken instrument, and an evaluation is only as trustworthy as what it measures with. The catalogue's expectations were never edited. Detection over hazards ended at 8 of 19; restoring the UNKNOWN contract did not widen the analysis envelope, and the eleven remaining silent hazards are untouched by it.

## Hazards EAL is silent about

These introduce moderate or severe real-world risk and produce no output at all. They define the true shape of the analysis envelope.

### M03 — DWB speed raised coherently, 0.26 -> 2.0 on controller and smoother

**Hazard:** severe

Same 8x speed increase on the DWB stack. DWB declares no prediction horizon, so the horizon rule does not apply and EAL is entirely silent. This is the clearest demonstration of the envelope's limit: a coherent, dangerous change on a controller without a horizon is invisible.

### M05 — Sensor marking range obstacle_max_range 2.5 -> 0.4 m (local costmap)

**Hazard:** severe

Obstacles beyond 0.4 m are discarded before reaching the costmap (obstacle_layer.cpp:552), so the robot cannot brake for what it cannot see. EAL extracts this value but no check consumes it. A severe hazard that is fully present in the config and still invisible.

### M06 — Inflation radius 0.70 -> 0.05 m

**Hazard:** moderate

The inflation layer is what keeps planned paths away from obstacles rather than grazing them. At 0.05 m the margin is below the costmap resolution, so paths may be planned essentially in contact with obstacles. EAL extracts inflation_radius and relates it to nothing, so the change is invisible.

### M07 — controller_frequency 20 Hz -> 2 Hz

**Hazard:** severe

Control period grows from 50 ms to 500 ms, adding 0.45 m of travel at 1 m/s before the next command. Extracted as a timing parameter, unchecked.

### M08 — Local costmap update_frequency 5 Hz -> 0.5 Hz

**Hazard:** moderate

Obstacle data becomes up to 2 s stale, so at 0.5 m/s the robot may act on a picture of the world a metre out of date. The value is extracted and typed as a timing parameter, but no check relates costmap freshness to the speed the robot is allowed to travel, so nothing fires.

### M10 — Braking weakened coherently on both sides, 3.0/2.5 -> 0.4

**Hazard:** severe

Stopping distance from 0.5 m/s grows from 0.05 m to 0.31 m, over 6x, and the two declarations agree perfectly. Coherent and dangerous.

### M12 — Angular limits converted to deg/s on both sides consistently

**Hazard:** moderate

A consistent unit error is arithmetically coherent. EAL has no notion of a plausible magnitude for a quantity, so nothing fires.

### M14 — Entire velocity_smoother section removed

**Hazard:** moderate

Documented as inapplicable rather than unknown: a stack legitimately may not run the smoother. Listed so the decision stays visible and arguable.

### M16 — Second controller plugin block with contradictory limits

**Hazard:** moderate

The importer takes the first plugin entry that resolves and never looks at the second, so a contradictory alternative controller is unexamined.

### M22 — Smoother claims 100 m/s^2 braking capability

**Hazard:** severe

Roughly 10 g of braking, physically absurd for a wheeled robot. Because the controller is now the conservative side, the only thing EAL says is a strict-only informational item. It has no notion of physical plausibility.

### M23 — Zero modelled actuation delay asserted alongside a 2 Hz control loop

**Hazard:** severe

The model asserts no actuation delay while the loop runs at 500 ms. EAL extracts both numbers and never relates them.


## Measurement notes

- **Actionability of emitted findings: 100%** — the share that name
  at least two source locations and a concrete fix. This is a proxy. **Human
  repair time was not measured**; that requires a human, and inventing a number
  would be worse than recording the gap.
- **Re-run agreement** compares two runs of every mutation in the same
  environment. Cross-environment reproducibility is covered separately by
  `make demo-check`, which is byte-for-byte from a clean checkout.
- Mutations are applied to a temporary copy; the fixtures are never modified,
  and their hashes are verified against `demo/provenance.json` before the run.
